# Full recruiter site (saved November 2026)

The complete version of brittanyhelm.com before it was simplified to a one-page landing page.

It had:
- **Home:** night Bay Bridge hero with headshot, stat boxes (8 years · People first · Custom AI · Any size),
  six expertise cards, five-role experience timeline, an AI workflows section (Design, Build, Set guidelines),
  education and career start, and a "Let's talk" contact section.
- **About:** portrait, career path, approach and background.

## How to restore it
- The exact version is commit `70088b5` on `main` ("Pause publishing until launch (#4)").
- Or copy the files in this folder back into `src/` (`pages/` → `src/pages/`, `components/` → `src/components/`,
  `styles/` → `src/styles/`, and `resume.ts`, `photos.ts`, `consts.ts` → `src/`).

Files in this folder are not built or published.
